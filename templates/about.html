About the Project

AI-Based Chocolate Paan Sales Prediction System designed to forecast sales volume, optimize inventory, and assist operational planning.

1. Project Overview & Objective

Project Title: AI-Based Chocolate Paan Sales Prediction System
Objective: Predict the continuous target variable Boxes_Sold using operational business, customer behavioral, social marketing, and external environmental factors.

Fresh confectioneries like Chocolate Paan suffer from perishable raw ingredients (betel leaves, cocoa fillings, sweet toppings). Underproduction leads to lost revenue and customer dissatisfaction during festive rushes, while overproduction leads to inventory spoilage. This project applies machine learning regression algorithms to accurately predict sales demand.

Target Variable: Boxes_Sold (Continuous integer representing boxes of chocolate paan sold on a given day).

2. Input Feature Space (14 Variables)

Dataset: Chocolate_Paan_Sales_Dataset_10000.xlsx (10,000 recorded instances).

Group Feature Name Data Type Business Description
Product & Pricing Temperature Float Ambient daily temperature (°C). Influences chocolate melting and consumer footfall.
Price Float Selling price per box in INR (₹). Governs price elasticity of demand.
Discount_Percentage Float Promotional discount offered (0% to 100%). Drives customer volume.
Marketing Campaigns Marketing_Budget Float Daily promotional spending in INR (₹) across online & offline ads.
Instagram_Reel Integer Number of Instagram Reels published today. Drives viral food discovery.
YouTube_Video Integer Number of food review & promo videos uploaded.
Facebook_Ads Integer Count of targeted Facebook sponsored ad sets running.
Customer Behavior Returning_Customers Integer Loyal customers visiting the store or re-ordering online.
New_Customers Integer First-time acquired consumers.
Customer_Rating Float Aggregated customer satisfaction rating (1.0 to 5.0 stars).
Order Distribution Online_Orders Integer Volume originating via food delivery portals (Zomato, Swiggy, Store App).
Offline_Orders Integer Volume originating via counter sales and physical kiosk walk-ins.
Calendar & Environmental Festival Binary (0/1) Indicator whether the day coincides with an Indian festival (Diwali, Holi, Eid, etc.).
Weekend Binary (0/1) Indicator whether the day is Saturday or Sunday.

3. Machine Learning Models & Evaluation Metrics

Evaluated Models (Week 5)

  • Linear Regression (Selected, R² = 0.9449)
  • Gradient Boosting Regressor (R² = 0.9161)
  • Support Vector Regressor (RBF Kernel, R² = 0.9001)
  • Random Forest Regressor (100 trees, R² = 0.8503)
  • Decision Tree Regressor (R² = 0.6174)

Evaluation Metrics

  • MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average magnitude of prediction errors in boxes.
  • MSE (Mean Squared Error): Average of squared errors.
  • RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Penalizes large individual errors.
  • R² Score (Coefficient of Determination): Proportion of variance in Boxes_Sold explained by the features.
🎓 Key Viva Discussion Point: Why did Linear Regression outperform Random Forest & Decision Tree?
The underlying business data shows a strong additive linear structure with well-behaved features. Decision Tree suffered from overfitting (Training R² = 1.00 vs Testing R² = 0.6174), because it partitioned feature space into rigid rectangular leaves without pruning. Linear Regression had virtually zero generalization gap (Training R² = 0.9402 vs Testing R² = 0.9449), demonstrating superior real-world reliability.
